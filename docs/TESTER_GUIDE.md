# Paws & Pedigrees: guide for testers

Thank you for trying the game. This is a test build: everything is playable, the numbers (prices, scores, how fast
dogs learn) are first guesses, and your reactions will decide what changes.

## What the game is

Your grandpa has left you his old championship kennel. You start with one rescue dog and build from there: train it,
take jobs, compete, restore the place, and breed the generations that follow. Dogs never die in this game; old dogs
retire to the farmhouse.

What you do with your hands matters. Dogs show what they are about to do with their ears, tail and nose, and your
timing and positioning change the result.

## Starting

- Open the link you were sent in Chrome, Edge or Safari, on a computer or a phone. Nothing to install.
- Your progress is saved in that browser on that device. A different browser or a private window starts fresh.
- Press **Begin**. The orange arrow and the goal card (top left) always show what to do next.

## Controls

| Computer                          | Phone                   | What it does                                                         |
| --------------------------------- | ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| W or up arrow; hold Shift to jog  | Push the stick up       | Walk forward                                                         |
| A and D, or left and right arrows | Push the stick sideways | Turn (the view turns with you)                                       |
| S or down arrow                   | Pull the stick down     | Step back                                                            |
| E                                 | The big orange button   | Use whatever you are standing at                                     |
| Click the ground                  | Tap the ground          | Send, throw, direct or "search here"; at home on a phone, walk there |
| Space                             | The big round button    | Whistle, "Yes!", "Go", "Lie down" (it says which on the button)      |
| R                                 | Call or Here!           | Call your dog                                                        |
| Drag, or Q                        | Drag                    | Look around for a moment                                             |
| Esc                               | Menu                    | Menu, the handbook, and tester shortcuts                             |

Every button on screen shows its key. **Menu → How to play** has a page for each activity.

## Two ways to test

**Play it properly (best for first impressions).** Start a new game and play the first week as a new player would.
Expect roughly an hour to reach the Village Fun Day on day 7. Please note the first moment you felt lost, and the
first moment you enjoyed yourself.

**Tour everything (best for finding problems).** Open **Menu** and use the tester shortcuts:

1. **Skip the first week** gives you a dog and opens everything after the Fun Day.
2. **+$200 and food** as often as you like.
3. **Train every dog to 75%** makes competitions winnable straight away.
4. **Skip a day**, **Skip to Saturday (shows)**, **Skip to Sunday (trial)**, **Skip to Cup day**.
5. **Age every dog a year** to reach retirement quickly.

## What there is to try

At home, walk up to anything with a ring on the ground:

- **Training field gate:** lessons (press "Yes!" at the moment your dog does the right thing), field work (retrieves
  with whistle and directions), and agility once you restore Grandpa's ring ($110).
- **Noticeboard:** paid jobs, mostly scent searches in Mara's orchard.
- **Kennel runs and food bowl:** feed your dogs; choose which one comes out with you.
- **Puppy paddock:** restore the whelping room ($80), plan a litter, raise the puppies, keep or place them.
- **Farmhouse door:** bed (ends the day), Grandpa's office (every dog's record, the kennel's reputation, letters,
  torn pages), and the trophy shelf and photo album.
- **The van** takes you everywhere else:
  - Competitions: field trials at Larkspur on Sundays; agility shows and sheepdog trials on Saturdays; the
    Hollowmere Cup on the last Sunday of winter (day 28, 56 and so on).
  - Fell Top Farm: work sheep with five commands from a post.
  - Sandy Cove: the beach, for water work or just a run on the sand.
  - The village: shop, vet, and the breeders' market on the first two days of each season.
  - Larchwood Rescue: more dogs (you have six runs).

Other things to look out for: the seasons change how the place looks; neighbours turn up at the gate; pages torn from
Grandpa's ledger appear as you restore the kennel and tell a story that ends with a new arrival; the **Photo** button
(top right) saves a picture to your album.

How a day works: a dog can work until it is worn out (the Energy bar, top left), and every dog has its own energy.
When one is tired, take another out at the kennel runs, or go to bed at the farmhouse door to start the next day.

## What we would most like to know

1. In the first ten minutes, did you know what to do? Where did you get stuck?
2. Which activity was the most fun? Which was the least, and why?
3. Did it feel as if your own skill made a difference, or were you watching the dog do it?
4. Breeding: did planning a litter, raising the puppies and choosing which to keep feel worth the time?
5. Anything that looked wrong, moved oddly, or was hard to see? A screenshot helps, with the day shown at the top.
6. On a phone: any button too small, hidden or in the way?
7. Would you play again tomorrow? What would bring you back?

When reporting a problem, please say: computer or phone, which browser, what you were doing, and what you expected.

## Known rough edges

- Prices, scores, rival strength and learning speed are first guesses.
- Characters have no facial expressions; people stand still when they are not handling.
- Sounds are simple tones (whistle, click, a woof); there is no music.
- Windows and doors are solid; you cannot go inside buildings (the farmhouse opens as a menu).
- The save lives in your browser. Clearing site data deletes it. **Menu → Delete save** starts again on purpose.
- There are no accounts, online play or purchases in this build.
